About

I study Physics Engineering at Istanbul Technical University and work on quantitative research. In summer 2026 I was the sole researcher on a ~6 TB full-depth order-book pipeline at Ata Portföy, where every claim had to pass a pre-registered test battery before it reached the desk.

Before that I modeled 0-DTE SPX option Greeks and trained a VAE for black-swan stress tests at Riskoptima Hedge Fund. I care about the unglamorous parts that make a result real: data validation, sealed holdouts, effective-N, and costs.
